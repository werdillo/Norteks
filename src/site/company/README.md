# company

Who Нортекс is: the company page («О нас») and the advantages that argue for the company. Static; the copy is in
`about.ts` and `advantages.ts`, the pictures in `assets/images/`. The advantage icons and the offer icons are UnoCSS
Tabler classes named in the data files.

The page: the introduction with its figures, the story («Кто мы») with the interior photos, «Что мы предлагаем»,
the advantages grid (`Advantages`, with `FeatureCard`), and a call to action above the footer.

Door (`index.ts`): `AboutPage`, `Advantages`, `headerNav`, `mobileNav`.
